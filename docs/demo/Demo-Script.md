# BatteryIQ client demo: narration

Video: `BatteryIQ_demo.mp4` (about 5 min 5 s, 1600 x 900, captions on screen, synthetic British English voice). Re-record with a different voice by changing the text below.

## 0. Title (0:00)

BatteryIQ. AI vision inspection for your vehicle battery plant. In the next few minutes, I will show you how it watches every battery, finds the cause of a defect, and helps your team fix it.

## 1. Control room (0:18)

This is the control room. In one screen you see line O E E, batteries inspected against plan, open alarms, first pass yield, and machines running. Below it is the live twin of your line, from grid casting to dispatch. Every machine is animated, and coloured by its live status. Click any machine to focus on it.

## 2. The four vision gates (0:42)

Every battery passes four A I vision gates, in order. Casing and appearance. Terminals and connectors. Cover, seal and assembly. And label, barcode and final appearance. These are exactly the four checks you asked us to cover. Each gate shows its pass rate over the last fifty batteries, and what is held.

## 3. Alarm and root cause (1:05)

Right now, gate Q3 is in alarm. The camera is finding incomplete seal beads. The system does not stop at saying something is wrong. It says that one hundred percent of the bad batteries came from platen two on the heat sealing machine, and it gives the fix. An unacknowledged alarm escalates to the shift in-charge, then the plant head. The operator acknowledges it, and opens the machine.

## 4. The machine in 3D (1:33)

Here is the heat sealing machine, in three D. Switch on the sensor tags, and platen two is reading low, exactly as the vision gate suggested. X-ray shows the heated platens inside. Below, the step of the cycle running now, and on the right, every live reading, the O E E, and the alarm to act on. The vision gate and the machine tell the same story.

## 5. Review and hold bay (1:59)

Back at the gate, doubtful batteries wait for a person. If the camera is not sure, the operator looks at the picture, and either releases it as a false alarm, or confirms the defect. Confirmed rejects go to the reject bay, to be scrapped, returned, or downgraded. Nothing is lost, and nothing is decided silently.

## 6. Fix and recover (2:22)

Once the cause is fixed, the operator applies the corrective action. The action is logged, new batteries pass, and watch the pass rate climb back above target. The alarm clears itself, and the cycle of detect, find the cause, fix, and confirm, is complete.

## 7. A second fault (2:49)

Let us try another problem. We inject a terminal weld defect at gate Q2. Within a few batteries, the gate reacts, the alarm is raised, and the machine behind it, the inter-cell welder, goes to attention.

## 8. Traceability (3:05)

Every battery is traceable. Pick any rejected battery, and see its lots, the tools it went through, and the pictures taken at each of the four gates. A defect traces back to the tool or lot behind it.

## 9. Copilot (3:20)

The copilot answers in plain English or Hindi, from the live data. Ask why the seal gate is failing, and it explains the cause, shows its sources, and offers buttons that open the gate or the machine. It runs on your plant server, so nothing leaves the site.

## 10. The AI, honestly (3:40)

We are also honest about the A I. This page shows how the camera's doubtful calls compare with your operators' verdicts, how those verdicts feed the next training set, and the targets that a pilot must prove, on your own labelled images, before go-live.

## 11. History and savings (3:58)

History shows each gate's pass rate by day against target, and the defect Pareto, with an export. And the savings page turns it into money, using your own assumptions, with the payback in months.

## 12. Roles and audit (4:13)

Access is controlled by role. Here, an operator tries to sign off the shift handover, and the app refuses. Only the shift in-charge or plant head can. Every action is logged with the person's name, in an audit trail.

## 13. Integrations and deployment (4:30)

It connects to your P L Cs, M E S, maintenance and quality systems, and the gates send reject, rework and hold signals to the line. The deployment page shows the cameras at each gate, the hardware, and a thirteen week rollout, from site survey to the first line live.

## 14. Close (4:51)

BatteryIQ catches the defect at the gate, finds the cause at the machine, and proves its value in your own numbers. Let us start with a pilot on one line. Thank you.
